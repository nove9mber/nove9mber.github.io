02 / SERVICES

What we do

We deliver complete builds or individual stages of work, tailored to your project.

  1. 01

    New builds

    Family homes, holiday homes and small apartment buildings, from excavation and foundations to handover. If you already have contractors for part of the works, we take on the rest and coordinate with them.

    More about this service
  2. 02

    Structural works

    Foundations, reinforced concrete structure, masonry, floor slabs and roof structure.

    More about this service
  3. 03

    Stone house renovation

    Old stone houses on the island usually have sound load-bearing walls, while the rest needs renewing. We clean and repoint the walls and install new ceilings, roof, services and floors; every stone that can be preserved stays where it is.

    More about this service
  4. 04

    Finishing works

    Facades, plastering, screeds, flooring, tiling and bathrooms.

    More about this service
  5. 05

    Retaining walls, terraces and pools

    Most plots on Krk are on a slope, so a retaining wall is a common part of the build. We clad it in local stone so it blends in with the surrounding drystone walls. We also build steps, terraces and pools.

    More about this service

06 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

All questions

Do you also take on smaller jobs?

Yes. Besides new builds, GRODEX does renovations, façades, bathrooms and retaining walls. Send a description and a few photos and we will let you know whether we can step in and when.

I live abroad. Can we work together remotely?

We can. We agree everything by phone, WhatsApp or email, and during the works we send photos from the site. You come to the island when it suits you.

How much does it cost to build a house on Krk?

That depends on the terrain, the design and the materials, so we do not give prices off the top of our heads. After the site visit you receive a written quote with a price for each item.
